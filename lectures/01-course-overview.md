## Learning goals

By the end of this lecture, students should understand:

- the main objectives of the course;
- why efficiency matters across the AI system stack;
- how algorithm, model, compiler, and hardware choices interact.

## Course philosophy

Efficient AI is not a single optimization technique. It is the coordinated design of models, software, runtimes, and hardware under constraints such as latency, memory, energy, and cost.

::: {.callout-important}
## Key idea

Treat efficiency as a **system-level design objective**, not only as a model-compression problem.
:::
